All items:

  • Compounding — Small consistent returns beat large occasional ones, given time.
  • Deliberate focus — Attention is directional, and what you rehearse you get more of.
  • Inversion — Ask what would guarantee failure, then avoid that.
  • Second brain — Knowledge you do not have to hold in your head to be able to use.
  • Start with why — The reason comes first, or the what never lands.
  • Systems, not goals — A goal is a state you might reach; a system is what you do repeatedly.
  • Zettelkasten — Small notes that link, so structure emerges instead of being imposed.
