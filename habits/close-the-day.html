Habit

Close the day before opening the next.

At the rollover: finish the previous day, file the loose captures, put the pointers in the index. A capture that never got linked is a capture that will not be found again.

It takes a few minutes. Skipping it does not save those minutes — it moves them to the search three weeks later, with interest.
