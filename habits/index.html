Habits

A rule says what should happen. A habit is the evidence of whether it does — the thing repeated often enough that it no longer needs deciding. Listed with their cadence, because a habit without one is an intention.

All items:

  • Close the day before opening the next. — Loose captures get filed, or they stop being findable.
  • Regenerate one image at a time, never a batch. — Generation is non-deterministic; --force overwrote an approved hero.
  • The daily note comes first. — Before status, before planning, before answering — read the day.
  • Verify on fresh output, not on what the script printed. — A build that reports success can still produce the wrong thing.
  • Walk without input sometimes. — Nothing joins up while something else is talking.
  • Write the decision down while the options are still warm. — The rejected options are the part that gets lost.
