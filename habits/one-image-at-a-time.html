Habit

Regenerate one image at a time, never a batch.

Image generation is non-deterministic. A batch run with `--force` replaced a hero that had already been approved with a duller one, and nothing in the output said so.

After approval: one at a time, look at it, then the next. The batch flag is for the first pass only.
