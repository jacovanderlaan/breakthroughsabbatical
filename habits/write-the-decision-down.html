Habit

Write the decision down while the options are still warm.

A decision record without its rejected options is a conclusion, not a decision. Six months on, the question is never *what did I choose* — it is *why not the other thing*, and that answer evaporates within days.

So the record carries the options and the rationale, written while both are still in the room.
