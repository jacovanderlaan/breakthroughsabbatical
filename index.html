Not a research sabbatical.

A breakthrough sabbatical. The difference is what the time is for. Not stepping back to study something, but taking a year on purpose: lay the foundations, clear out what a couple of busy decades left lying around, and get properly rested. In that order on paper; all at once in practice.

Clear the old to make room for the new.

Three modes, all at once

The tidy version is: decompress first, build later. Nobody actually lives like that, and it wastes the good half of the year. So these run side by side, roughly equal thirds, and rest is not the one that gets squeezed.

Foundation

Write it down. Don't launch it.

Ideas, offerings, drafts, half-formed income models — all captured properly and then deliberately left alone. Turns out "not yet" is a strategy and not just procrastination with better branding.

Catching up

What the busy years left behind

The clear-out, the paperwork, the wills nobody enjoys writing, the doctor's appointments that kept sliding. Nothing here will ever make a highlight reel. It is most of the point.

Rest

Beach, no hurry, good films

A full third. Not the leftover, not the reward for finishing the other two, but a mode in its own right, with the diary space to prove it.

What fills it

The attention that a year like this frees up. Some rooms are furnished, some are still marked out on the floor.

Practice

What I've tried on myself

GTD and the productivity shelf on one side; Abraham Hicks, Eckhart Tolle, Robbins and Landmark on the other. Plus the four things I actually believe.

 Things

Things worth owning

Clothing, interiors, personal care. Scandinavian minimalism as a filter rather than a look — and what I deliberately avoid.

 Reading

The other shelf

Monocle, Gestalten, Taschen. Design, cities and the wanderlust that paper feeds rather than cures.

 Rotterdam

The city

Not just an address. A city that was rebuilt as a decision instead of inherited as a past — and the places I keep returning to.

 Stage

Contemporary dance

NDT, Introdans, Holland Dance, Nationale Opera & Ballet. The one art form that happens once and cannot be backed up — which is the whole attraction.

 Film

Arthouse

French, Italian and Spanish. LantarenVenster is the home screen, Picl and Pathé Thuis do the rest.

 Music

Trance, disco, and no apologies

The dancefloor half. Trance and progressive at the core, Modern Talking on the indefensible shelf, Ibiza as the address.
Still a frame in places. A site earns its work when there is something to put on it, not before. The soon cards are marked out but not built — they fill up as the material arrives, not to make the page look finished.

How this works

There are four places under my name. The line between them is not the subject — it is systems versus content, and this one is the year the content got made.
