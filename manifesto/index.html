Manifesto

This is the year.

Take it before you have earned it.
Nobody hands it over, and the right moment does not arrive.

Do the boring half first.
The paperwork, the doctor, the drawer that has not opened since 2019.
None of it will make a highlight reel. Most of it is the point.

Write it down. Do not launch it.
“Not yet” is a strategy, not procrastination with better branding.

Rest is a third of the work, not what is left over.
If it is the part that gets squeezed, you did not take a sabbatical.
You took a long weekend with ambition.

Build something you did not have to.
Then use it. Then fix it. Then use it again.

Say the thing out loud before it is finished.
Half of it is wrong and you will only find out that way.

Structure beats magic.
Not because structure is impressive, but because magic does not scale
and you are one person.

Keep the receipts.
A year you cannot remember is a year you did not have.

Do not come back the same.
That is the whole idea.

Take the time.

Written in the form of the Holstee Manifesto (2009), which has been doing this job for longer and better.
