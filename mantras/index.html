Mantras

Not principles — those tell you what to do and can be violated. A mantra is what survives when there is no time to reason: short, imprecise on purpose, and there to be recalled at the moment of choosing. Most of these are borrowed. That is the nature of the form.

All items:

  • mantra-clear-the-old — Clear the old to make room for the new.
  • mantra-lost-in-the-right-direction — It feels good to be lost in the right direction.
  • mantra-not-yet-is-a-strategy — Not yet" is a strategy.
  • mantra-rest-is-a-third — Rest is a third of the work.
  • mantra-structure-beats-magic — Structure beats magic.
  • mantra-take-the-time — Take the time.
