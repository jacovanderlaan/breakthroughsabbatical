What I tell myself

Mantras

Not principles — those tell you what to do and can be violated. A mantra is what survives when there is no time to reason: short, imprecise on purpose, and there to be recalled at the moment of choosing. Most of these are borrowed. That is the nature of the form.

All items:

  • Clear the old to make room for the new. — The paperwork, the drawer, the appointments that kept sliding.
  • It feels good to be lost in the right direction. — A paradox, and that is where it works: lost and right direction do not belong together.
  • Not yet" is a strategy. — Writing something down and deliberately not launching it is a decision, not procrastination.
  • Rest is a third of the work. — Not the reward for finishing the other two, and not what is left over.
  • Structure beats magic. — The clever one-off does not scale and you are one person.
  • Take the time. — The year does not start when you have earned it, because that day does not arrive.
