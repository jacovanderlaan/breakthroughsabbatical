Compounding

The financial idea applied to everything with a time axis: knowledge, structure, relationships, health. What matters is not the size of one deposit but the number of them and how long they are left alone.

The uncomfortable corollary is that the early curve looks flat, which is exactly when people stop. A vault is unimpressive for two years and then is not.
