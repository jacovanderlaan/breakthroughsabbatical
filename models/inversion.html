Inversion

"All I want to know is where I'm going to die, so I'll never go there." Working backwards from failure is often sharper than working forwards from success, because failure modes are concrete and success is vague.

This is why the anti-patterns get their own section rather than a footnote under Patterns. What we refuse is usually clearer than what we hold.
