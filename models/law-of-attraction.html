Deliberate focus

Stripped of the metaphysics, the usable part is this: attention is not neutral. What you rehearse mentally is what you notice, and what you notice is what you act on.

I keep it in the list because it is load-bearing for the energy behind the work — no-nonsense and positive, forward rather than defensive — even though I would state its mechanism differently than its source does.
