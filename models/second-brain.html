Mental model

Second brain

An external system that holds what you know, structured well enough that retrieval is cheap. The point is not storage — it is that thinking gets better when recall stops competing for the same attention.

What I took from it: capture is the easy half. The structure that makes something findable two years later is the half that gets skipped.

Where I diverge: Forte's PARA organises by actionability. My spine is the calendar, because almost everything I want to find again, I remember by roughly *when*.
