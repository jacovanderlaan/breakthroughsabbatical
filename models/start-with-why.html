Mental model

Start with why

People do not buy what you do, they buy why you do it. In practice: any explanation that opens with the mechanism loses the room before it reaches the point.

I use this structurally, not just rhetorically. Every ADR opens with Context before Decision, for the same reason — the decision is unreadable without the problem it answers.
