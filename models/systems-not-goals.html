Mental model

Systems, not goals

"You do not rise to the level of your goals, you fall to the level of your systems." A goal is reached and then leaves you without a next move; a system keeps producing.

This is the same reason I write intentions rather than goals: an intention describes a direction, and a direction cannot be missed by a deadline.
