Mental model

Zettelkasten

One idea per note, each note linked to its neighbours. No master hierarchy decided up front — the structure is what the links turn out to be.

What I took from it: the atomic unit. A note that says one thing can be reused; a note that says six things can only be read.

This is why concepts, rules, mantras and habits are separate types rather than sections of one document. Each is addressable, and now each has a URL.
