Rule

Getting lost will help you find yourself.

Travel often, and be willing not to know where you are. The same argument as the print about being lost in the right direction, which is probably why both ended up on the wall.

Where it comes from: a line from the Holstee Manifesto (2009), which hangs framed on the wall. Quoted with attribution — the poster's text is Holstee's, and the full thing is not reproduced here.
