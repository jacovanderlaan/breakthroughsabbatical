Rule

If you don't like something, change it.

Not advice — a statement that the option exists. Most of what stops a change is the quiet assumption that you need permission, and this is someone saying plainly that you do not.

Where it comes from: a line from the Holstee Manifesto (2009), which hangs framed on the wall. Quoted with attribution — the poster's text is Holstee's, and the full thing is not reproduced here.
