What I hold myself to

Personal rules

A principle is professional and general — it holds for anyone doing the work. A personal rule holds for one person, and is written down precisely because it would otherwise get quietly dropped. Collected here rather than written as one block: a manifesto is what these become, not what they start as.

All items:

  • rule-build-what-you-do-not-need — Build something you did not have to.
  • rule-do-not-come-back-the-same — Do not come back the same.
  • rule-do-the-dull-half-first — Do the dull half first.
  • rule-getting-lost-helps-you-find-yourself — Getting lost will help you find yourself.
  • rule-if-you-dont-like-it-change-it — If you don't like something, change it.
  • rule-keep-the-receipts — Keep the receipts.
  • rule-life-is-simple — Life is simple.
  • rule-rest-does-not-get-squeezed — Rest does not get squeezed.
  • rule-say-it-before-it-is-finished — Say it out loud before it is finished.
  • rule-stop-over-analysing — Stop over-analysing.
  • rule-write-it-down-dont-launch-it — Write it down. Don't launch it.
